However, I know that no country is perfect and that there are always people who will disobey the law. On another note, I think that perhaps people who come to Singapore are more likely to throw their trash on the ground if it isn't something enforced in their home country.

2. Do people like being a policeman in your country?

I would say so. Actually, my father is a police officer and he seems to enjoy it quite a bit, although it is a tough job. Since Singapore has a reputation to maintain, the police force works extremely hard to give their all to make sure that Singapore remains among the safest countries. That said, some police officers feel quite a lot of pressure, which can make it challenging but rewarding.

3. What qualities should a police officer have?

First, I believe that a police officer must have a high sense of integrity. As corruption is a problem in many countries which prevents progress in a place, it is important that the police officer has a sense of integrity along with a sense of right and wrong, honesty, and a genuine concern to make the country safer. Also, they must be hard-working and not too relaxed as there are always people trying to break the law without getting caught of the law. All in all, they must be a strict person to be efficient.

4. Why some people want to become a lawyer?

Honestly, I think some people choose it for the money, as it is a well-paid job in many countries. Second, some people feel quite passionate about the law and want to dedicate their life to it. Third, there are many different branches of law, such as international, elder-law, criminal, etc., so there are many different opportunities within this career path.
