WHY I PREFER LIVING IN AN APARTMENT

🎧 Today, I'd like to talk about why I prefer living in an apartment.

🎧 I've been living in an apartment for quite a while, and overall, I think it's a really convenient way of living, especially because I work full-time and have a fairly busy schedule. Of course, apartment living isn't perfect, but personally, I still prefer it to living in a traditional house.

🎧 The main reason is convenience. In my apartment building, there are quite a few facilities nearby. There's a supermarket, some convenience stores, cafes, and other services around the building. There's even a swimming pool, so I can exercise without having to travel somewhere else.

🎧 I also like the fact that everything is relatively close together. If I need to buy some food or pick something up after work, I can usually do it within a few minutes. After a long day at work, I really appreciate that kind of convenience.

🎧 Another advantage is security. Compared with living in a house, I feel that an apartment building generally has better security. There are security guards, cameras, access cards, and other security measures. I also know that if there's a problem with the building, I can contact the management team instead of dealing with everything by myself.

🎧 However, there are some disadvantages. The biggest one is that you don't have as much privacy or personal space as you would in a house. You may also hear noise from neighbors. Another issue is that you're more dependent on the building management. For example, if an elevator or shared facility has a problem, you have to wait for the management team to fix it. There are also usually rules about renovating or changing your apartment.

🎧 Even so, I think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages for me. Since I work full-time, I value convenience more than having a large amount of private space. Maybe I'll change my mind in the future, but for now, apartment living suits me quite well.

HOW I USUALLY SPEND MY WEEKEND

🎧 Today, I'd like to talk about how I usually spend my weekend.

🎧 For me, the weekend is mainly a time to relax, recharge, and spend some quality time doing things that I don't have time for during the week. Since I work from Monday to Friday, I usually look forward to the weekend quite a lot.

🎧 My weekends are actually pretty simple. I don't usually make a detailed plan. I just try to have a good balance between relaxing, exercising, spending time with my wife, and doing some personal things.

🎧 On Saturday morning, I usually get up a little later than I do on weekdays. After breakfast, I normally play badminton for about two hours. It's a good opportunity to get some exercise and have fun with other people. I enjoy badminton because it's much more enjoyable than simply working out by myself.

🎧 After that, I go home, have lunch, and take some time to rest. By then, I'm usually a little tired, so I don't want to do anything too demanding. Sometimes I watch videos or a TV show, and sometimes I just use my phone or take care of some personal things.

🎧 Saturday evening is usually when I go out with my wife. We might go to a shopping mall, have dinner somewhere, or simply walk around and look at different shops. One place we often go to is AEON Mall Bình Tân because it's quite close to where we live. We don't necessarily have to do anything special. Sometimes just going out together and having a meal is enough.

🎧 Sunday is usually more relaxed. I might do some housework, organize my things, and study English. For example, I might listen to English podcasts or practice speaking. If I have enough energy, I may also go swimming or do another kind of exercise.

🎧 In the evening, I try to relax and get ready for Monday. I might prepare some things for work and check my schedule.

🎧 Overall, my weekends aren't particularly exciting, but I actually like them that way. After a busy working week, I don't need to travel somewhere or do something special. A good weekend, for me, is about balance: getting enough rest, staying active, spending time with people I care about, and starting Monday feeling refreshed.

WHY TRAFFIC IS SUCH A BIG PROBLEM IN HO CHI MINH CITY

🎧 Today, I'd like to talk about why traffic is such a big problem in Ho Chi Minh City.

🎧 If you live and work in the city, traffic is probably something you have to deal with almost every day. During rush hour, some roads can become extremely crowded, and a journey that normally takes 30 or 40 minutes can take much longer.

🎧 I think the first major reason is the large number of vehicles on the roads. Ho Chi Minh City has a very large population, and many people use private vehicles, especially motorcycles. Motorcycles are convenient because they're relatively cheap, easy to park, and can get through small streets. However, when a huge number of people travel at roughly the same time, the roads can quickly become overcrowded. The number of cars has also increased, and cars take up much more road space.

🎧 Another problem is that the city's infrastructure hasn't always been able to keep up with its rapid growth. Some roads are quite narrow, especially in older areas. There are also busy intersections where traffic can easily build up. When there's road construction or an accident on a busy road, it can create a traffic jam that affects a much larger area.

🎧 Personally, I find the journey home quite mentally tiring. After spending eight hours at work, the last thing I really want to do is sit in traffic. Sometimes you can spend a long time moving only a short distance, and that can be very frustrating. Traffic also affects people's quality of life because long commutes can cause stress and tiredness. There's also air pollution from vehicles and noise from engines and horns.

🎧 I think one of the most important solutions is better public transportation. The city needs a system that is convenient, reliable, and easy to use. More metro lines and better connections with buses could encourage more people to leave their motorcycles or cars at home.

🎧 However, I don't think building more roads alone will solve the problem. The city needs better infrastructure while also encouraging people to change the way they travel.

🎧 Overall, I don't think traffic can be solved overnight. Ho Chi Minh City is growing quickly, and transportation improvements take time. But if the city continues to invest in public transportation and infrastructure, I hope getting around the city will gradually become less stressful.

THE BENEFITS OF PLAYING SPORTS EVERY WEEK

🎧 Today, I'd like to talk about the benefits of playing sports every week.

🎧 I think staying active is really important, especially for people who spend most of their day sitting at a desk. If you have an office job, you can easily spend eight hours in front of a computer, and after work, you may just want to go home and relax. For me, playing sports is a good way to stay active without feeling like I'm doing a boring workout.

🎧 The first and most obvious benefit is physical health. Playing sports regularly can improve your fitness, strength, and endurance. It can also help you maintain a healthy weight and give you more energy during the day. You don't necessarily have to play for several hours. Even one or two sessions a week can be a good start. The important thing is to make physical activity part of your routine.

🎧 The second major benefit is mental health. This is especially important for people with stressful jobs. When you're working all day, you have to concentrate, solve problems, answer messages, and meet deadlines. After several hours of that, your mind can become quite tired.

🎧 Playing sports gives you a chance to take a break from all of that. When I'm playing a sport, I have to focus on what I'm doing at that moment. I'm not thinking about work or other problems. In that sense, exercise helps me clear my mind. I also usually feel better after exercising. Even if I'm physically tired, I often feel more relaxed and refreshed mentally.

🎧 Another benefit is the social aspect. Some sports are much more enjoyable when you play with other people. You can meet new people, spend time with friends, and have some friendly competition. For example, when I play badminton with a group of friends, I'm not just exercising. We're also talking, laughing, and having fun together.

🎧 However, many people find it difficult to exercise regularly. The most common reasons are probably lack of time, tiredness, and motivation. Some people also think they need to do intense workouts every day, which can make exercise feel like a chore.

🎧 Personally, I think it's better to find an activity that you actually enjoy. You don't have to go to the gym every day or run ten kilometers. You could play badminton, go swimming, ride a bicycle, or simply go for a walk.

🎧 Overall, I think regular sports are good for our physical, mental, and social well-being. You don't need to become an athlete. Just find something you enjoy and do it consistently. For me, consistency is more important than intensity.

THE ADVANTAGES AND DISADVANTAGES OF ONLINE LEARNING

🎧 Technology has become such a normal part of my life that sometimes I don't even notice how much I depend on it.

🎧 If I think about a normal day, I use technology from the moment I wake up until I go to bed. I use my phone, my computer, the internet, and different apps for work, communication, entertainment, and learning. Overall, technology has made my daily life much more convenient, although there are definitely some downsides.

🎧 First of all, technology has changed the way I start my day. My smartphone is usually one of the first things I check in the morning. I might look at my messages, check the weather, or see if there are any important notifications. It also helps me during my commute. Instead of just sitting there doing nothing, I can listen to English lessons, podcasts, or other useful content. This lets me turn otherwise wasted time into learning time.

🎧 Another big change is the way I work. Since I work in IT, technology is obviously a huge part of my working life. I spend a lot of time using a computer, writing code, communicating with colleagues, and searching for information online. If I run into a technical problem, I can usually search online and find documentation or an example that helps me solve it. Communication at work is also much easier because I can send a quick message, share a file, or join an online meeting.

🎧 Technology has also changed the way I communicate with other people. Nowadays, I can contact my family or friends almost instantly. I can send a text message, make a video call, or send a photo or voice message. This is especially useful when people live far away. However, because communication is so easy, we sometimes spend too much time on our phones instead of talking face to face.

🎧 Another important area is entertainment and learning. I can watch videos, listen to music, and browse the internet whenever I want. I also use technology to improve my English. I can listen to podcasts, read English texts, and use online tools to practice. The biggest advantage is that I can learn whenever and wherever I want, at my own pace.

🎧 Technology has also made many small everyday tasks easier. I can order food, shop online, make digital payments, and use maps to find places. Each thing may seem small, but together they can save a surprising amount of time.

🎧 However, technology isn't completely positive. It's very easy to get distracted by notifications or spend too much time scrolling. We can also become too dependent on our devices.

🎧 So, for me, the important thing isn't to avoid technology. It's to learn how to use it wisely. Technology is a great tool as long as we stay in control of it and use it to make our lives easier, more productive, and hopefully a little better.
