I live in an apartment complex called Vision, in Tan Tao Ward, Binh Tan District.
I bought my apartment about a year ago, and I've been living there ever since.
Overall, I'm really happy with it, and I would say it's a pretty comfortable place to live.
The whole project is divided into three phases.
So far, only the first phase has been completed, and it consists of four buildings, which are named A, B, C and D.
Two of them are 25 storeys tall, while the other two have 19 floors.
The remaining six buildings are still only at the foundation stage, so I think it'll be quite a while before the entire project is finished.
Once the whole project is completed, there will be ten buildings altogether.
I live on the ninth floor of Building A, which is one of the 25-storey buildings.
My apartment is not particularly large, but it's comfortable enough for me and my wife.
One thing I really appreciate is that I have a fairly open view from my balcony, so the apartment doesn't feel too cramped.
After living there for about a year, I've gradually become quite attached to the place.
Each building has two elevators.
To be honest, the elevators have broken down quite a few times since I moved in, which has been a bit frustrating.
A lot of residents also complained that the elevators got extremely hot, especially during busy hours.
Fortunately, the management recently installed some extra fans inside, so they're much more comfortable now.
It's a small improvement, but it actually makes quite a noticeable difference in daily life.
One of my favourite things about living here is how spacious the apartment complex is.
There are wide internal roads, a nice park with lots of trees and benches, and a large pond right in the middle of the complex.
I often go for a walk there in the evening because it's relatively quiet, and it's a great way to unwind after work.
Actually, one of the nicest parts of the complex is the large open space in the middle of the four buildings.
It's built slightly higher than the internal roads, and there are a few sets of stairs leading up to it.
The area is filled with benches, so it's a very popular place for residents to relax.
In the evenings, the whole area becomes quite lively.
Kids run around and play together, while adults sit on the benches, chat with their neighbours or simply enjoy the fresh air.
I think this creates a really friendly atmosphere.
Even though we live in an apartment complex, it doesn't feel completely isolated or impersonal.
You can see people interacting with each other, which I think is quite nice.
The facilities are also pretty convenient.
There are two convenience stores on the ground floor, WinMart and Co.op Food, so buying groceries is usually very easy.
If I only need some basic food or household items, I don't have to go anywhere else.
I only go to GO An Lac or AEON Mall Bình Tân when I need something that these stores don't have or when the prices are noticeably cheaper there.
The cafés downstairs are another nice feature.
They're usually quite busy in the evenings, with people stopping by for coffee, chatting with friends or even working on their laptops for a while.
Personally, I don't go to them that often because I usually prefer staying at home after work, but I like having the option nearby.
The apartment complex also has a large swimming pool, and the best part is that it's free for residents.
I think that's a really good facility, especially considering how hot the weather can be in Ho Chi Minh City.
It's a nice place to cool off on hot days or spend some time with family at the weekend.
Because the complex has quite a lot of open space, there's even a basketball court.
On normal days, people use it to play basketball, especially in the evenings.
During special occasions, such as International Children's Day on June 1st, the court is sometimes transformed into an event area.
Tents are set up and parties are organised for children.
It's always quite fun and lively, and a lot of families join in.
Another thing I really like is the location.
It's only about a seven-minute drive to GO An Lac and around ten minutes to AEON Mall Bình Tân, so it's really convenient whenever I need to go shopping or grab something to eat.
I think location is particularly important when you buy an apartment because you're going to live there for many years.
So, having supermarkets and shopping centres nearby makes everyday life much easier.
The area around the apartment complex has also improved quite a lot recently.
In the past, the road leading to the complex was often flooded whenever there were high tides.
That was quite inconvenient, especially for people commuting to work.
But after both sides of the Tham Luong Canal were reinforced, flooding has become much less of an issue.
The road used to be crowded with street vendors as well, which made driving in and out a bit frustrating.
Thankfully, most of them have been cleared away, so getting around is much easier now.
I think these changes have made the neighbourhood feel more organised and more comfortable than it was when I first moved in.
Another thing I appreciate is the security.
There are quite a lot of security guards around the complex, and they seem to do their job very well.
They're usually present at the entrances and in different areas of the complex, so I generally feel quite safe living there.
For me, that's especially important because I often come home after work in the evening.
Of course, living there isn't perfect, and there are a couple of practical issues that residents often talk about.
The biggest one is probably parking.
The complex only has one basement parking area.
It's large enough for bicycles and motorbikes, but there isn't enough space for cars.
Even the parking spots along the internal roads are completely full, and the waiting lists for both the basement and the internal-road parking are extremely long.
Last time I heard, each list had more than a hundred people waiting for a parking slot.
So, parking is definitely one of the major weaknesses of the apartment complex.
There are also a few minor problems with insects.
Because the complex has a lot of open space and greenery, there are quite a lot of mosquitoes around the park in the late afternoon.
On rainy days, rove beetles sometimes fly into the apartment through the balcony or windows.
So I have to be a little careful and make sure they don't get inside.
But honestly, these are relatively minor issues compared with the advantages.
Overall, I really enjoy living in Vision.
I like the spacious layout, the facilities and the convenient location.
More importantly, I feel that the area is gradually developing, and the quality of life is improving over time.
So, if I had to describe my home in one sentence, I would say it's not a perfect place, but it's a comfortable and convenient place where I can genuinely see myself living for quite a long time.
