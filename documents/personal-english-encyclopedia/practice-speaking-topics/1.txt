HOBBIES

→ My favorite hobbies and interests

When it comes to my hobbies, I would say that I have a few different interests rather than just one particular hobby. 
One of my favorite things to do is playing badminton because it allows me to stay active while also having fun. 
I usually play with my friends or colleagues, and I really enjoy the competitive but friendly atmosphere. 
Apart from sports, I also enjoy listening to English podcasts and watching English-speaking videos because I’m trying to improve my listening and speaking skills. 
Sometimes, I also like playing games when I have some free time. 
I wouldn't say that I play games all day, but they are a good way to take my mind off work. 
So overall, my hobbies are a mixture of physical activities, entertainment, and things that help me improve myself. 
I think having different hobbies is important because it gives me more ways to enjoy my free time.

→ When I usually do them

The time I spend on my hobbies depends quite a lot on my work schedule. 
Since I work from Monday to Friday, my weekdays are usually quite busy, so I don't have a lot of free time during the day. 
After work, I normally have dinner, take a short break, and then spend some time on my personal interests. 
For example, I might listen to English content while commuting because that doesn't require me to set aside extra time. 
In the evening, if I'm not too tired, I might practice speaking English, play a game, or watch something for entertainment. 
Weekends are different because I have much more flexibility. 
I usually have more time for outdoor activities, especially badminton. 
I prefer doing physical activities in the morning because the weather is usually more comfortable and I feel more energetic at that time. 
So basically, I try to fit my hobbies around my daily routine instead of treating them as something separate from my life.

→ How often I enjoy them

I enjoy my hobbies on a regular basis, but the frequency depends on what kind of hobby I'm talking about. 
For example, I try to play badminton at least once a week because it's one of the activities I enjoy the most. 
I don't necessarily need to play every day, but having a regular session gives me something to look forward to during the week. 
When it comes to English, I actually spend time on it almost every day because I'm trying to make steady progress. 
I might listen to English in the morning, practice speaking in the evening, or watch English videos when I have some free time. 
As for gaming and other forms of entertainment, I usually do them less frequently because they aren't really a priority for me. 
I think the key is finding a balance. 
I don't want my hobbies to take up all of my free time, but I also don't want to completely give them up because they make my life more enjoyable.

→ Who I usually do them with

Some of my hobbies are things I enjoy doing by myself, while others are much more enjoyable when I have someone to do them with. 
For example, I prefer playing badminton with friends or colleagues because the social aspect makes the activity much more fun. 
We can talk, joke around, encourage each other, and sometimes even have a little friendly competition. 
I think that's one of the reasons why I keep coming back to it. 
On the other hand, activities like listening to podcasts, studying English, or watching videos are things I usually do alone. 
I actually enjoy having some quiet time because I can completely focus on what I'm doing without being interrupted. 
Sometimes I also think that hobbies can help strengthen relationships. 
If you regularly do an activity with someone, you naturally spend more time together and get to know each other better. 
So for me, it really depends on the activity. 
Some hobbies help me socialize, while others give me some valuable time for myself.

→ How I became interested in them

I became interested in my hobbies at different stages of my life, and there wasn't really one specific moment when I decided to have them. 
For example, I became interested in badminton because it was an easy sport to pick up and I could play it with people around me. 
At first, I just played casually for fun, but after playing several times, I started to enjoy it more and became more interested in improving my skills. 
My interest in English developed in a different way. 
Because I work in IT and sometimes communicate with people from other countries, I gradually realized that English could be very useful for both my career and my personal life. 
At some point, I stopped thinking of English as just a school subject and started seeing it as a practical skill. 
That changed the way I learned it. 
I began listening to podcasts, watching videos, and practicing speaking more regularly. 
So I think many of my hobbies started naturally rather than being something I deliberately planned from the beginning.

→ Why I enjoy doing them

There are several reasons why I enjoy my hobbies. 
First of all, they give me a break from my normal routine. 
Work can sometimes be repetitive or mentally demanding, so doing something completely different helps me reset my mind. 
I also enjoy hobbies that give me a sense of progress. 
For example, when I practice English and notice that I can understand something more easily or express myself more naturally, it makes me feel like my effort is paying off. 
The same thing happens with badminton. 
When I improve a particular technique or play better than I did before, I feel a small sense of achievement. 
Another reason is that hobbies give me something to look forward to. 
Even if I've had a stressful week, I know that I'll have some free time to do something I enjoy. 
I think that's really important because life shouldn't be only about work and responsibilities. 
Having activities that genuinely make you happy can make your daily life feel more balanced and enjoyable.

→ How my hobbies help me relax

My hobbies are one of the main ways I deal with stress after a long day at work. 
When I've been sitting in front of a computer for several hours, doing something physical like badminton helps me completely change my state of mind. 
Instead of thinking about work, deadlines, or technical problems, I have to focus on the game and react to what's happening in front of me. 
That helps me forget about work for a while. 
Even hobbies that are less physical can help me relax. 
For example, listening to something interesting or playing a game allows me to switch my attention to something enjoyable. 
Sometimes I don't even need to do anything particularly exciting. 
I can simply sit down, put on my headphones, and listen to something while relaxing. 
I think the important thing is that hobbies give my brain a chance to step away from work. 
They create a clear separation between my professional life and my personal time, which helps me recharge and come back to work with a fresher mind.

→ What I can learn from them

I think hobbies can teach us much more than we initially expect. 
For example, playing badminton has taught me about patience, concentration, and consistency. 
You can't become good at a sport just by playing once or twice. 
You have to practice regularly, make mistakes, and gradually improve. 
That experience has helped me understand that progress usually takes time. 
Learning English has taught me something similar. 
Sometimes I study for weeks without feeling that I'm making much progress, but when I look back after a few months, I can clearly see the difference. 
My hobbies have also taught me how important it is to enjoy the process rather than only focusing on the final result. 
If I only cared about becoming better, I might get frustrated easily. 
Instead, I try to enjoy what I'm doing and let improvement happen naturally. 
So in a way, hobbies can teach us valuable life skills such as discipline, patience, time management, and the ability to keep going even when progress is slow.

→ My most memorable hobby experience

One of my most memorable experiences related to my hobbies was playing badminton with a group of friends and colleagues on a weekend. 
What made it memorable wasn't necessarily the game itself, but the atmosphere around it. 
We weren't playing professionally or anything like that, but everyone was really enthusiastic and wanted to do their best. 
There were moments when we made ridiculous mistakes and laughed about them, and there were also some surprisingly intense rallies where everyone became really competitive. 
After playing for a while, we were exhausted but felt really satisfied. 
We went somewhere afterward, had something to eat, and talked about all kinds of things. 
Looking back, I think that's what makes hobbies special. 
Sometimes the most memorable experiences aren't the ones where you achieve something impressive. 
They're simply moments when you spend time with people you enjoy, have fun, and forget about your everyday responsibilities for a few hours. 
That's the kind of experience I would like to have more often.

→ How much time they require

The amount of time my hobbies require varies quite a lot. 
Some hobbies can be done in just a few minutes, while others require me to set aside a couple of hours. 
For example, I can listen to an English podcast during my commute without having to spend any additional time on it. 
That's quite convenient because I can combine learning with something I already have to do. 
Playing badminton, on the other hand, requires more planning. 
I usually need to spend around one or two hours for a session, including the time it takes to travel there and get ready. 
I try not to let my hobbies take over my schedule, especially on weekdays. 
I have work, household responsibilities, and other things to take care of, so I need to manage my time carefully. 
At the same time, I don't think hobbies should be something we squeeze into the tiny amount of time left after everything else. 
I think it's worth deliberately making time for activities that are important to us.

→ How much money they cost

Another interesting thing about hobbies is how much money they can cost. 
Fortunately, most of my hobbies aren't particularly expensive. 
For example, listening to podcasts or practicing English can be almost free, especially because there are so many free resources available online. 
Playing badminton costs a little more because I need equipment and sometimes have to pay for a court. 
However, compared with some other hobbies, I still think it's relatively affordable. 
I don't believe that an expensive hobby is necessarily a better hobby. 
In fact, some of the most enjoyable activities require very little money. 
You can go for a walk, read a book, listen to music, exercise, or simply spend time with friends without spending much at all. 
Of course, some people enjoy hobbies like photography, traveling, collecting things, or playing musical instruments, which can become quite expensive. 
Personally, I prefer hobbies that give me a lot of enjoyment without putting too much pressure on my budget. 
I think the value of a hobby should be measured by how much happiness or benefit it brings, not simply by how much money we spend on it.

→ Indoor hobbies versus outdoor hobbies

I enjoy both indoor and outdoor hobbies, but they give me completely different experiences. 
Indoor hobbies are usually more convenient because I can do them at home regardless of the weather. 
For example, I can watch a movie, listen to a podcast, practice English, play a game, or read something whenever I have some free time. 
I don't need to travel anywhere, so they're perfect when I'm tired or simply want to stay at home. 
Outdoor hobbies, on the other hand, require more effort, but they also give me the chance to be physically active and get some fresh air. 
Playing badminton is a good example. 
After spending most of my working day indoors and in front of a computer, I actually appreciate having an activity that gets me moving. 
If I had to choose between the two, I wouldn't want to give either one up. 
I think the best approach is to have a balance. 
Indoor hobbies are great for relaxation and convenience, while outdoor hobbies are better when I want to exercise, socialize, and get away from my usual environment.

→ My hobbies during weekdays

During weekdays, I usually choose hobbies that are easy to fit into a busy schedule. 
Since I have to work during the day, I don't have enough time for activities that require several hours. 
One of the things I often do is listen to English during my commute. 
This is particularly convenient because I can turn otherwise unused time into learning time. 
After I get home from work, I usually take some time to rest and have dinner. 
If I still have enough energy, I might spend some time practicing speaking English, watching something interesting, or playing a game. 
I try to avoid doing anything too demanding late at night because I don't want my hobbies to affect my sleep. 
On weekdays, I think the main purpose of my hobbies is to help me relax and maintain a good balance. 
I don't necessarily need to accomplish something every evening. 
Sometimes simply doing something enjoyable for thirty minutes is enough. 
The important thing is to have some personal time after spending most of the day working.

→ My hobbies during the weekend

My weekends are when I have the most freedom to enjoy my hobbies. 
Since I don't have to follow my normal work schedule, I can spend more time on activities that require a little more preparation. 
One thing I particularly enjoy doing on weekends is playing badminton, usually in the morning. 
I prefer the morning because I feel more energetic and the weather is generally more comfortable than later in the day. 
After that, I might have breakfast or coffee with friends or colleagues and spend some time chatting. 
The rest of the weekend is usually more flexible. 
I might stay at home, watch something, play games, practice English, or simply relax. 
I also try not to make every weekend extremely busy. 
Sometimes I deliberately leave a few hours completely free because I think having nothing planned can also be relaxing. 
For me, a good weekend isn't necessarily one where I do lots of exciting things. 
It's one where I have enough time to do what I want, spend time with people I care about, and recharge before another working week begins.

→ Hobbies I want to try someday

There are also several hobbies I'd like to try someday. 
One of them is learning to play a musical instrument, perhaps the guitar. 
I've always thought it would be really interesting to be able to pick up a guitar and play a song instead of just listening to music. 
Another hobby I'd like to explore is photography. 
I think photography is a great way to pay more attention to the world around you because you start noticing small details, interesting places, and beautiful moments that you might normally ignore. 
I'd also like to travel more and perhaps turn traveling into a more regular hobby rather than something I only do occasionally. 
The reason I haven't started some of these hobbies yet is simply that I don't have unlimited time. 
I already have work, exercise, English practice, and other responsibilities. 
However, I don't think I need to start everything at once. 
I can gradually try new things whenever I have more time. 
I believe trying new hobbies is a good way to keep life interesting and discover new sides of yourself.
