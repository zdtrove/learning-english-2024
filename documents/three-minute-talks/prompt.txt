3-minute talk - [topic]

Create a 3-minute English presentation on this topic.

First, create a clear and concise key outline with numbered sections (1, 2, 3, etc.).
The outline should be a memory framework for speaking, NOT a summary of the full presentation.

For each section:

* Give it a short, clear title.
* Include several short keywords or phrases representing the main ideas.
* Put each bullet point on a separate line.
* Keep the bullet points short and simple.
* Avoid long or detailed sentences.
* Do not include unnecessary details.

Then provide:

1. A natural, conversational English presentation of about 3 minutes based on the outline.
2. A list of useful English phrases and collocations from the presentation that are worth learning.

The presentation should be around 350–450 words and should sound natural and conversational, suitable for both English speaking practice and giving a short presentation in front of a small group.
Use spoken English rather than overly formal or essay-like language.
Focus on developing ideas naturally rather than trying to include too many points.
The outline and presentation should NOT be identical. The outline is for remembering the structure and key ideas, while the presentation is the natural English version that I can practice speaking.
